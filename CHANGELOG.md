# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-29

First release.

- Build dealerships in game: a sales desk, showcase spots, and where bought cars and test drives come out
- Cars, luxury, motorcycles, trucks, boats and aircraft, each with its own cars, prices and stock
- Run by nobody, or by a job with commission for the salesperson
- A window sticker for every car, with performance read from the car itself
- Pick the paint when you buy. Cars are otherwise sold stock
- Pay with cash, bank or finance
- Finance payments come out of the bank on their own; missed ones end in the car being taken back
- Swap the car on a showcase spot for any other the dealership sells
- Timed test drives
- A dealer tablet for staff: stock, ordering, the business account, sales and the team
- Works with most banking, keys, fuel, garage and inventory resources, found on its own
- /dealerdiag prints what the resource found and why something might not work
